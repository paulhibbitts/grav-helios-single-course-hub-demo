# Syllabus

**Introduction to Teaching Online** (ITO) · Non-credit · 5 weeks, about 10 hours

## About the Course

This course gives you a foundation for teaching online. It is mostly asynchronous, with three one-hour live sessions. By the end, you will have started planning your own online course with the Backward Planning template, guided by the Understanding by Design framework.

## Course Outcomes

- Considering the online context, create and organize learning outcomes
- Select and use effective online formative and summative assessment strategies
- Select and use effective online instructional approaches that align with outcomes
- Reflect on your experiences as an online learner and how they will shape your online teaching

## Course Outline

| Week | Module | Activities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Welcome and Introductions | Live meet and greet · Competencies for online teaching · Share your online learning experiences |
| 2 | Online Context and Learning Outcomes | Planning pre-assessment · Backward planning · Write your own course outcomes and learning objectives |
| 3 | Assessment Strategies | Assessment pre-assessment · Live check-in · Align assessments and activities with outcomes |
| 4 | Instructional Approaches | Instructional approaches pre-assessment · Select effective instructional strategies |
| 5 | Reflections and Practice | Live reflection session · Post-course self-assessment · Share your reflections |

## Live Sessions

- **Week 1** – meet and greet, a tour of the course, and sign-up for discussion board moderators
- **Week 3** – check-in on progress and questions
- **Week 5** – reflections on learning online and what they mean for your teaching

Weekly drop-in office hours are also held over Zoom.

## Discussion Boards

Each module has a discussion board. Keep your first post to one paragraph, and reply to at least one other post. Post by Wednesday each week, so moderators can respond by Friday. Modules 2–4 use small groups, while Modules 1 and 5 are whole-class discussions.

## Time Commitment

Plan on about two hours per module. You can work at your own pace, but keeping to the weekly schedule makes it easier to connect and collaborate with others.

## Getting Help

Use **I need help** in the Welcome module, or send a message through the course Inbox. Replies come within 24 hours on weekdays and 48 hours on weekends.
