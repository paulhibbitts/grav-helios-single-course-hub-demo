# Syllabus

**GPV – Digital Animation** (ANIM8) · Instructor: D. Ingram

## About the Course

An introductory course in the basics of animation using Adobe Animate. The emphasis is on project-based, hands-on practice – developing skills and storytelling by making a series of short films. If you have a particular skill you want to explore, we can develop a personal project around that interest.

Each module starts with a short video, reading or demo that explains the concepts you need for its projects.

## Big Ideas

- The design cycle is an ongoing reflective process
- Personal design choices require self-exploration, collaboration, evaluation and refinement of skills
- Design and content can influence the lives of others

## Competencies

- **Applied design** – understanding context, defining points of view, ideating, prototyping, testing, making and sharing
- **Applied skills** – identifying the skills needed for specific projects
- **Applied technology** – choosing the appropriate tools and technologies for tasks

## Course Outline

| Module | Focus | Assignments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | Welcome, expectations and getting help | – |
| Module 1 – Basic 2D Objects | Drawing objects and backgrounds in Animate, and simple animation with Motion Tweens | A1 Drawing vs Shapes · A2 Creating Multiple Objects · A3 Basic Animation |
| Module 2 – Beyond Basic 2D Animation | Storytelling: sound, Scenes, frame-by-frame movement such as walk cycles, and backgrounds | A4 Sound Bite · A5 Face Expressions · A6 Reaction · A7 Walk Cycle |
| Module 3 – Final Project | A short film on a subject of your choice | A8 Final Project |

## Assessment

| Category | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Assignments (A1–A7) | 70% |
| Final Project (A8) | 20% |
| File Management (F1 in week 1, F2 in week 5) | 10% |

## Tools

- **Adobe Animate**, part of Adobe Creative Cloud, for all course projects
- **Audacity** for sound work
- A drawing tablet is optional – most of the work can be done without one, and a limited number are available to borrow

## Expectations

Be responsible, courteous and cooperative with everyone in the class, and take responsibility for your own learning. Keep your project files organized – the File Management tasks check this in weeks 1 and 5.

## Getting Help

Questions about the course or technical problems? See **Questions and Tech Help** in the Introduction module, or ask in class.
